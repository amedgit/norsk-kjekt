Norwegian that people actually use

Textbook Norwegian is not enough when you need to order lunch, talk to a landlord, understand a work email or make a doctor's appointment. Norsk Kjekt mixes structured lessons with the everyday phrases you hear in Oslo and across Norway.

Everything is in bokmål, with audio recorded in a clear eastern Norwegian pronunciation. The interface can be used in English, Spanish or Persian, while the Norwegian you learn stays the same.

How to use it in 20 minutes a day

  1. 5 minutes of vocabulary. Review your flashcards and keep the streak alive.
  2. 10 minutes of course. Do one lesson, one grammar topic or one Norskprøve exercise.
  3. 5 minutes of listening or speaking. Repeat a short dialogue or try the pronunciation game.

If you only have two minutes, Norsk Sprint gives you ten quick questions, a combo and three stars. It is designed to make starting easy, not to punish you for missing a day.

Preparing for Norskprøve

The mock exams follow the real structure: reading, listening, writing and speaking. You get a timer, a result by skill and model answers for the writing and speaking tasks. Use the Norskprøve guide to understand the levels and what each section asks of you.

Free, private and offline

There are no accounts, no paywall and no ads inside the lessons. Your progress, theme and language preference stay in your browser. You can export a JSON backup and import it on another device. Install the PWA to keep the core course available offline.

Frequently asked questions

Is Norsk Kjekt really free?

Yes. There is no paywall, no account and no trial. The course is free and your progress stays in your browser.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the site and start. Progress is saved locally on your device, and you can export or import it as a backup.

Which Norwegian variant do you teach?

The course teaches bokmål, the written form most learners and most people in Norway use. The audio is based on an eastern Norwegian pronunciation.

Can I prepare for Norskprøve?

Yes. There are full A1, A2, B1 and B2 mock exams with reading, listening, writing and speaking sections, a timer and a report by skill.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Norsk Kjekt is a PWA. Install it from your browser and the core lessons, audio and exercises keep working without a connection.
